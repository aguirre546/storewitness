Last updated: 12 November 2026

StoreWitness is an experimental store-status tool by Victor Aguirre. A status is a recent observation, not a promise that a store can serve you. Check with the store before an important trip.

What you can expect

Camera readings may be wrong, unclear, delayed or unavailable. Owners can set temporary overrides or breaks. Three matching readings and timestamps help explain a signal; they do not guarantee accuracy. Demonstrations and replay data are labelled. Google Maps updates, two-camera CCTV and continuous background monitoring are not current features.

Use cameras and store information responsibly

Only connect a store or camera you own or are authorised to manage. Obtain any required permission from the store and people affected. Do not connect public street cameras without permission, secretly monitor people, identify individuals, publish personal details or use the service for harassment or unlawful activity. Keep private pairing tokens secret. You are responsible for information and reasons you submit.

Public information and your content

Anyone with a store's public link can view its name, status, reasons, times and retained activity. Enter business information only. You keep your rights in information you provide and allow StoreWitness to process and display it as needed to run the feature you request. We do not claim ownership of your camera footage.

Do not use it for emergencies, security decisions or situations where an incorrect result could cause serious harm. Keep your own backup of important business information.
