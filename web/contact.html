Contact Us

Last updated: 12 November 2026

StoreWitness is created and maintained by Victor Aguirre. Our current support channel is GitHub public support. There is no contact form, support inbox or live chat on this website.

Report a bug or suggest an improvement

Describe what happened, what you expected, your browser and device, and the steps to reproduce it. A screenshot is useful after removing private information.

Open a GitHub support issue

You may need to sign in to GitHub. Issues are public and use GitHub's own privacy policy.

Privacy, removal or sensitive security concerns

Do not post a private pairing token, personal details, camera footage, a private store link or a security exploit in a public issue. For a sensitive concern, start with a general request for a suitable private follow-up channel through Victor Aguirre's GitHub profile. No secure private support inbox is currently published. Do not send sensitive information until a suitable channel is agreed.

An incorrect store status?

Contact the business directly before travelling. StoreWitness cannot guarantee that staff are present or a service is available. Owners can use a labelled override or break to explain temporary changes.

Before sharing a screenshot

Hide pairing tokens, personal information, faces and anything confidential. Support is handled when available; no response time is promised. This is not an emergency or security-response service.
