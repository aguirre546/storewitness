01 / OBSERVE

A designated indicator.

The operator’s browser checks a coded OPEN or CLOSED marker. This prototype does not infer business activity from crowds, lighting or arbitrary objects.

02 / CONFIRM

Wait for consistency.

Three matching observations confirm a status. Brief obstruction and conflicting readings stay uncertain. Camera failure is not treated as closure.

03 / SHARE

A useful public signal.

Customers see the observation, source and timestamp. Owners can temporarily override it. Customers never need the operator’s private pairing token.

HONEST BY DESIGN

A working prototype with a defined scope.

Live capture needs a supported webcam, permission and an open operator page. Replay boards are explicitly labelled test data. This app does not modify Google Maps, verify that staff can serve you, or guarantee a store’s availability. A real deployment needs the owner’s permission and field testing.
