Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

StoreWitness is operated by Victor Aguirre. This notice explains the current community prototype. It is not a guarantee of perfect security. Use Contact Us for support and privacy-request instructions.

Information used by the service

  • Store information: Store ID, store name, camera/replay source, status, confidence, reasons, timestamps, owner overrides and break end times.
  • Pairing: A private token allows owner controls. The service stores a hash of it and, if enabled, a hash of a separate recovery code; the active browser tab holds the entered token. It is not included in public status links or history downloads.
  • Camera and images: Frames are processed on your device by this site's browser code. The application does not send or save camera frames, uploaded images or audio in its status database. Browser permission is required for a camera. Object checks count supported classes; this prototype does not perform face identification.
  • Connection data: Cloudflare handles requests and may process IP addresses and request/security data to host and protect the site. The app uses an hourly hash derived from the connecting IP for creation-rate limits, rather than saving that raw IP in a store board.

Trip watch and public branch settings

Owners may publish a business address, coordinates and links to participating Store IDs. These are public; do not enter private home details. Trip watch runs in the current visible browser page and does not request or store your current location. Sound and spoken alerts are optional. Watch choices are not saved after reload. Alternatives use linked stores with fresh OPEN status; distances are straight-line from the destination. Opening directions sends the chosen business destination to Google Maps under its policies. No background push subscription or automatic rerouting is enabled.

Why it is processed

Information is used to deliver the features you request, share status with the public-link audience, authenticate owner actions, limit abuse and maintain the service. Camera permission and optional device storage are user choices. Necessary service and security processing continues when optional storage is declined. Reasons and store names must not contain personal or sensitive details.

Who can see it and outside providers

Store names, status, reasons, times, source and retained activity are visible to anyone who has the public Store ID or link. Treat this as public information, not a confidential record. Cloudflare Pages and D1 host the website and database. When you choose to load scanners, model files are downloaded from services such as jsDelivr, Google-hosted model storage and Project Naptha. Those providers receive connection information, including your IP address, under their own policies. Model loading is separate from cookie/storage acceptance. Processing may occur outside the Philippines.

GitHub hosts source code and public support. Opening those links uses GitHub's own privacy practices. We do not sell store information or use advertising trackers in the application. No optional visitor analytics is enabled by the application. Cloudflare hosting, service metrics and security processing remain.

Cookies and device storage

The application does not set advertising cookies. The cookie manager covers similar device storage too. It remembers Accept or Decline in local storage for 180 days. Accept allows opening hours to be saved locally per Store ID; Decline keeps hours for the current page session and removes previously saved hours. You can reopen Manage cookies in the footer at any time. Refusing optional storage does not block core status, history or camera controls. Hosting/security cookies, if supplied by Cloudflare, follow its policies and are not switched off by this control.

Retention and security

Each store keeps its latest 200 activity events; older events are discarded as new ones arrive. Boards and their current metadata have no automatic expiry in this prototype and remain until removed by the operator. Rate-limit buckets are bounded to the latest 5,000 entries, not a fixed retention period. Device hours stay until removed, replaced or cleared; expired permission prevents their use until you accept again. You can replace the token and create a single-use recovery code while connected. Recovery replaces both credentials. A downloaded private recovery file contains credentials and must be kept private. Archiving a store pauses changes and keeps history; it does not erase records. Pairing is lost when its active tab closes or reloads unless you saved the details privately. Browser or operating-system clipboard history is outside our control.

HTTPS, hashed pairing tokens and access checks reduce risk, but no service is completely secure. No camera video archive is maintained. Do not submit secrets or unnecessary personal information.

Your choices and privacy requests

You may request information about your data, correction, access, objection or erasure where applicable, and may raise concerns with the Philippine National Privacy Commission. Use Contact Us to start a request. Our present support channel is public GitHub, so post only a general request for a suitable private follow-up channel; do not post identifying data, footage, private tokens or proof of identity. A secure private contact process is not yet provided by this prototype. We may need to verify authority before changing a store. Responses and deletion are handled manually and are not instant.

See National Privacy Commission guidance, Cloudflare's policy and GitHub's policy for further information.
