FOR PARTICIPATING ESTABLISHMENTS

Your camera.
Your store’s latest signal.

Customers check the public website on their phones. Your establishment runs the local StoreWitness Connector on a computer connected to its camera.

1. Get the establishment app

Open connector source and setup instructions ↗. Download the repository ZIP, extract it, and open its connector folder. Install Python 3.11 or later and run Start-Connector.ps1 -Install. The local dashboard opens at http://127.0.0.1:4332 on that computer.

2. Connect a store and camera

Create a camera board in Operator studio and privately copy its Store ID and a newly created connector key into the local connector. Choose USB webcam or an owner-provided RTSP/RTSPS stream from a compatible IP camera or DVR/NVR. A proprietary cloud-only CCTV camera may not be supported. Use a separate replay board for the built-in sign demo.

3. Check the sign and hours

Set Philippine opening hours and select the sign rectangle. Test OPEN, CLOSE and CLOSED before enabling monitoring. The connector runs local text recognition. It does not assume a store is closed because its lights are off or no people are visible.

4. Share the customer page

Start monitoring in the local app, then share your public customer URL. Customers need no Python installation or CCTV access. They see timestamped status and confirmation; the private camera address and footage are not displayed.

What must stay running?

The connector and establishment computer must remain on. The setup tab can close; the Python process must stay running. Sleep, shutdown or camera/internet failure interrupts updates. The website becomes Unavailable after observations expire, unless a labelled owner override remains active. This version does not install an automatic-start service.

Privacy and testing limits

Frames are processed locally and are not recorded or uploaded. Status metadata goes to StoreWitness. Optional saved credentials use Windows user encryption; keep the connector’s private folder off GitHub. Physical camera compatibility, field accuracy, continuous operation and recovery must be tested at each establishment. This is a one-camera prototype, not a verified integration with every CCTV system.
