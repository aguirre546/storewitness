1. Source and models

Models download from their public CDNs. Image processing runs on this device; frames are not uploaded. Initial loading needs internet. Supported objects are limited to COCO-SSD classes, not every object or a trained shutter detector.

3. Send observations to your camera board

Use a camera board from Operator studio. Three matching, acknowledged observations ten seconds apart confirm a public label. Pairing authenticates the sender; it cannot independently prove the camera evidence is true.

Observations stay on this page until sending is enabled.

What this does and does not detect

Object detection draws boxes for supported common objects, including people. OCR reads visible English text; brightness describes the selected frame, not the store’s electrical lights. A person can be out of view and a camera can adjust exposure. This experimental route does not ingest RTSP CCTV or combine two feeds. Do not use it as a guarantee of service.
