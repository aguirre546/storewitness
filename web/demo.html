TRY A DIFFERENT SITUATION

Accelerated explanation: each stage takes 4 seconds here. In the working prototype, observations are spaced ten seconds apart and become stale after 35 seconds.

01 / OUTSIDE

Watch the entrance.

A store-authorized camera could observe a selected shutter or entrance region. A camera mounted outside the store can be used; public street-camera access is not assumed.

02 / INSIDE

Check the store’s signal.

A second store-owned camera could read a designated OPEN/CLOSED indicator. Lights, a person, or an empty room alone cannot establish whether customers can be served.

03 / DECIDE

Require consistent evidence.

This animation proposes a two-camera agreement rule. Matching signals count toward confirmation. Conflicting or unreadable signals stay uncertain; stale feeds become unavailable.

DEMONSTRATION ≠ LIVE CCTV CONNECTION

A clear vision.
A defined working prototype.

Working today: one webcam reads supplied coded markers, uses three matching checks, and shares a timestamped status page. Replay tests and owner overrides are labelled.
